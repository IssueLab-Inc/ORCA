ORCA identity 01 - quality review
30 October 2026

VISUAL REVIEW
Reviewed the rendered asset overview, all four guide pages, all six vector
print-master pages, and the dark/light social, square, banner and video layouts.
PDF pages were rendered with Poppler. A clear-space label was moved below the
diagram border and page 2 was rendered and checked again.

The final artwork follows approved direction 05. It is a manual vector
reconstruction of the concept, with deliberately defined curves and cuts;
the generated reference PNG has not been traced into raster-filled SVGs.

The O in the wordmark and the standalone standard symbol share identical path
commands. The micro version changes only the diagonal cut width, from 18 to 24
master units. The source ring is 256 units tall and the clear space is 64 units.

AUTOMATED REVIEW
The build checks PNG decoding, RGBA transparency and clear-space bounds;
SVG parsing, outlined text and absence of bitmap embeds; PDF reopening, page
counts, extractable text and absence of raster images; all manifest icon paths
and dimensions; all seven ICO sizes; shared O geometry; the central 80 percent
maskable-icon safe circle; sRGB profiles on exported PNGs; and ZIP integrity.
manifest.json lists file sizes, dimensions where relevant and SHA-256 hashes.

PNG assets are rasterised from SVG at twice their target resolution and then
downsampled with sharp. Transparent logo exports keep genuine alpha, including
the white variants. Social/video cards have intentional solid backgrounds.

EPS files contain plain vector PostScript commands from the same master paths.
They use RGB, as do the print PDFs. The files are not PDF/X or a CMYK proof.

This records the original standalone delivery. Repository-edition commands and
validation are documented in README.md and docs/brand-assets.md.
